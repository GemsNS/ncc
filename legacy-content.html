About Us (Legacy Text)

From nccfamily.us

Thank you for taking the time to find out more about New Community Church and our community. Our ministry was established in Suffolk, VA in 1997. We hope that after you look through our website and attend a worship service, you will consider becoming a more active part of our growing community of faith.

Join us every Sunday at 11:00 AM and Wednesday at 7:00 PM via Zoom.

Our church is not just about a weekly service on a Sunday morning or an occasional Bible study. We are a community of faith sharing our lives together throughout the week as well.

Our common faith in our Lord Jesus paints the way we do life together and is the undercurrent of all our social interactions. We share our joys, successes and good times. We also share our hurts, disappointments and failures.

Ultimately, Christians are merely followers of Jesus. We aspire to His teachings, we are taught by the Bible, and are daily surrendering our lives to the guidance of the Holy Spirit.

We look forward to taking the journey with you.

Our Pastor (Legacy Summary)

Public Summary

Rev. A.M. VanDyke is Pastor and Founder of New Community Christian Center in Suffolk, Virginia.

He is a native of the Tidewater area and serves with a focus on practical biblical growth and transformed living.

He graduated from Smithfield High School, earned a degree in Education from Chowan University, and a degree in Pastoral Ministries from Liberty University.

His teaching emphasizes practical biblical principles, including finances and abundant living in Christ.

Online Giving (Legacy Text)

From Online Giving Page

First: We believe in tithing. The tithe is 10% of any income/increase God blesses us with. We believe the tithe belongs to God and should be returned to Him (Malachi 3:10).

Second: We believe in sowing financial seeds to demonstrate gratitude for God’s favor, goodness, mercy and grace (Luke 6:38).

Finally: We believe in giving to our pastor so he can focus on studying the Bible and hearing from God to pour into us weekly (Galatians 6:6-10).
