About NCC

A Community Of Faith

New Community Church was established in Suffolk, Virginia in 1997. We exist to help people know Jesus, grow in faith, and build meaningful Christ-centered relationships throughout the week.

Pastoral Leadership

Pastoral Leadership

Rev. Anthony M. VanDyke, Pastor and Founder, faithfully leads New Community Church in Suffolk, Virginia, with a passion for practical Bible teaching, spiritual growth, and transformed living.

A proud Tidewater native, Pastor VanDyke is committed to equipping believers to grow in faith, walk in purpose, and experience measurable progress in every area of life. Alongside his wife, Faith VanDyke, he serves the church and community with dedication, compassion, and vision

A Community of Faith

Our church is not just about a weekly service on Sunday morning or an occasional Bible study. We are a community of faith sharing our lives together throughout the week as the Body of Christ.

Our common faith in Jesus shapes how we do life together. We share joys and victories, and we walk through hurts and disappointments in Christian hope and unity.

Our Identity

We are followers of Jesus who are committed to biblical teaching, worship, and practical love for our neighbors.

Our Rhythm

Sunday worship and midweek connection create a steady rhythm for spiritual growth and shared life.

Join us every Sunday at 11:00 AM and Wednesday at 7:00 PM.
