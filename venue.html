About Our Spaces

Conveniently located in Suffolk, Virginia, New Community Church (NCC) has served the Hampton Roads area since 1997. Our campus is available for wedding ceremonies, receptions, banquets, memorials, meetings, and community gatherings.

Our facilities feature well-maintained indoor spaces and welcoming grounds at 5112 Godwin Blvd, offering a comfortable, elegant environment for events of many sizes.

Experience flexible layouts, clear acoustics, and a stage area well suited for worship celebrations, presentations, and photography.

Premium Amenities

  • Sanctuary seating for large worship and special events
  • Hospitality areas for families and wedding parties
  • Kitchen facilities for catering and fellowship meals
  • Sound, projection, and livestream-capable media systems
  • Ample paved, well-lit on-site parking
  • Outdoor areas for community events (seasonal)
