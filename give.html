Giving Principles

First

We believe in tithing. The tithe is 10% of any income/increase God blesses us with. We believe the tithe belongs to God and should be returned to Him. By obeying this principle, we remain in position to receive open-window provision from God (Malachi 3:10).

Second

We believe in sowing financial seeds to demonstrate how grateful we are for the favor, goodness, mercy and grace God continually expresses toward us. As a result of this principle, we also know we will receive a return (Luke 6:38).

Finally

We believe in giving to our pastor. By making sure his natural needs are met, we allow him to focus on studying the Bible and hearing from God so that he can pour into us weekly (Galatians 6:6-10).
